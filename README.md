The purpose of the Admitted Student’s Day testbed was to evaluate the stability and performance of the inner-loop flight controller under control conditions for Admitted Students Day. This would allow students to visually see how the PID controller works to stabilize the drone when manual torque is applied. Under successful testing conditions, the drone would demonstrate its ability to self-stabilize when subjected to disturbances meant to simulate disturbances from gusts of wind. The project involved connecting the drone to a PVC pipe which was mounted between two vertical stands. The pipe could be torqued gently, rotating the drone and simulating wind from two directions.
